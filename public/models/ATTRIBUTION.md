Models created by the NIAID 3D Printing and Biovisualization program. Large intestine geometry is from Stony Brook University (Arie Kaufman); brain geometry derives from the Allen Human Reference Atlas.

Human_Surface.glb adapts VH_M_Skin.glb with a neutral pelvic surface and removes external genital detail. Other included GLB files preserve their source geometry. The urethra is a simplified internal outlet. Corpus adapts their scale and materials at runtime, applies cutaway shading, and adds illustrative organ motion. Surrounding vessels, nerves, muscles, bones, some smaller organs and animated process diagrams are simplified educational geometry. This assembled application is not a clinically validated anatomical atlas. Flow colors and motion timing are teaching conventions.

Version 4 registers supporting bones, vessels, nerves and muscle groups to the reference A-pose and constrains their resting surfaces to a sampled body envelope. Muscle fibers and bone grain are procedural teaching textures. Joint movement is shown separately in process close-ups. Optional audio is synthesized and illustrative; it is not a clinical recording.
